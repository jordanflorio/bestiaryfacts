The Bestiary

Astonishing animal facts — and every one is cited to a real scientific source.

The natural world is full of quiet marvels, and the truth is stranger than anything we could invent. A seahorse where the father gives birth. A woodpecker whose tongue wraps all the way around its skull. A sloth that climbs down from its tree just once a week.

The Bestiary makes short, beautiful animal videos. Each one ends with the source it came from — a journal paper, not a listicle — so you can check it yourself.

How these videos are made

We think you should know how the sausage is made, so:

  • Every fact is drawn from a published source, which is named on screen. Facts are taken from the source text itself, never from a language model's recollection.
  • The narration is an AI-generated voice, licensed for commercial use.
  • Footage is licensed stock or Creative Commons material, with attribution where the licence requires it.

About "Bestiary Publisher"

Bestiary Publisher is the private software we use to upload our own finished videos to our own accounts through each platform's official API. It is not a product, it is not available to anyone else, and it never touches anyone else's data. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what it does and does not do.
